Our Story

Why We Built LibraryOS

In 2019, our founder visited a school library in Sylhet where the librarian was managing 8,000 books with three handwritten registers and a 10-year-old notebook. A book had been lost for 2 years — no one knew who had it.

We looked at existing library software. Most was designed for large Western universities — too complex, too expensive, and entirely in English.

So we built LibraryOS from the ground up — for schools and libraries in Bangladesh and similar contexts. Simple enough for any librarian to learn in a day, powerful enough to handle 50,000 books.
